About

A designer who ships.

I've spent the last several years between design tools and code editors, which is where I do my best work.

I started as a front-end developer, moved into product design, and never quite let go of either. That means when I hand over a design it's usually already running in a browser.

I care about the boring parts: empty states, error messages, keyboard navigation, and what happens on a phone with a bad connection. Those are the moments where a product either respects its user or doesn't.

Most projects I take on are small and specific — a marketing site, a dashboard, a design system that needs rescuing. I like working directly with the people who will live in the thing I'm building.

How I work

  • Start with the constraint. Budget, timeline, and the one thing that must work.
  • Show something early. Rough screens in week one beats polished screens in week six.
  • Leave it maintainable. Plain files, clear names, a README that explains itself.
